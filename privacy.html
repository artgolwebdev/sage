Privacy Policy

SAGE Tattoo Studio · Eilat Street 22, Tel Aviv-Yaffo · Last updated 25 November 2026

This website is a static site. There are no accounts, no login, and no database — nothing you type into the booking form is sent to or stored on this website.

What happens to your booking request

The booking form runs entirely in your browser. When you confirm, it opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. We only see that message once you send it from your own WhatsApp account, and we keep it only as long as needed to arrange your appointment.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages people visit and how they found us. It is not loaded until you press “Accept” in the consent banner, and you can decline. We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking pixels. Decisions you make are stored in your browser's localStorage under the key sage_cookie_consent.

To withdraw consent, clear this site's data in your browser settings — the banner will appear again on your next visit.

Embedded content

  • Google Maps — our contact section embeds a Google map, so Google may set its own cookies when the page loads. Google's privacy policy applies to that content.
  • Google Fonts — our body font is served by Google, which receives your IP address when the font is requested.
  • Instagram and WhatsApp — links to these services leave this site and are governed by their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • No registration, no newsletter, no user profiles.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with data brokers.
  • No photo uploads — reference images are sent directly through WhatsApp.

Your rights

You can ask us what booking information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by messaging or calling +972 52 650 4348. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

We tattoo clients aged 18 and over only. This website is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update date at the top of this page changes with it.
